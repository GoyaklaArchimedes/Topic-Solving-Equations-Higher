# GCSE Solving Equations Mastery — Higher

**Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition** · GCSE single-topic series

A complete interactive Higher course in solving equations, taught with Mr Nathan's Wall & Escalator method: recognise the structure of an equation, choose the quick move, then check the answer.

**Open it:** https://goyaklaarchimedes.github.io/Topic-Solving-Equations-Higher/

## What's inside

- 25 sections and 60 taught skills: Mr Nathan's Wall & Escalator method, what an equation means, one-step and two-step equations, brackets, fractions and decimals, unknowns on both sides, forming equations from words, perimeter and area, angle equations, tables and multi-step contexts, graphical solutions, simple quadratics, linear simultaneous equations, forming simultaneous equations, harder linear equations (including no solution and identities), harder quadratics and exact roots, completing the square, the quadratic formula and discriminant, algebraic fractions and restrictions, quadratic geometry problems, advanced simultaneous equations, non-linear simultaneous equations (line and parabola, line and circle), numerical and graphical methods, iteration, plus equations with a common base (extension)
- Every section has Learn, Interactive Test, Printable Practice and Progress pages
- Worked examples with step-by-step solutions, a hint for every question and a "Why it works" explanation
- New numbers every time, with recent questions remembered so practice does not repeat
- Answers can be typed as whole numbers, decimals or fractions; quadratics accept roots in either order
- Printable sets of eight questions with separate worked-solution sheets
- A balanced 50-question Mastery Test (two questions from every section), on screen or printed, with a section-by-section breakdown

## Level

Higher. Includes all the Foundation methods, then the advanced Higher methods. Suitable for AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and IGCSE.

## How to use it

Open the link in any web browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or interactive whiteboard. There is nothing to install, and no app, account or subscription is needed. Progress is saved on the device.
